Bond network (property)

This page documents an OPTIMADE Property Definition. See https://schemas.optimade.org/ for more information.

ID: https://schemas.httk.org/defs/v0.1/properties/chemistry/bonds
Definition name: bonds

Property name: Bond network
Description: The bond network of a structure: which atoms are bonded to which, optionally the shape of the bonds around the atoms of each site, and who assigned them.
Type: dictionary
Implementation requirements:
- Support: OPTIONAL support in implementations, i.e., MAY be null.

  • Query: Support for queries on this property is OPTIONAL.
  • Response:

The network is stated in terms of the served sites of the structure, so a client needs only fractional_site_positions (or cartesian_site_positions and lattice_vectors), site_coordinate_span and, for a symmetric span, space_group_symmetry_operations_xyz. What counts as a bond is decided by whoever assigned the network, named in source.

Requirements/Conventions:

  • The value MUST be null when no bond network is known, and when site_coordinate_span is not one of unit_cell, molecular_unit_cell, fundamental_domain, molecular_fundamental_domain, asymmetric_unit and molecular_asymmetric_unit.

  • Otherwise it MUST be a dictionary with the following keys:

    • pairs: REQUIRED; List of dictionaries. The bonds; an empty list means the structure is known to have no bonds. Each dictionary has the keys site and partner, 0-based indices into the sites of the structure (the order of species_at_sites and fractional_site_positions), and operation_xyz, an affine operation on fractional coordinates. The atom at fractional_site_positions[site] is bonded to the atom at the point obtained by applying operation_xyz to fractional_site_positions[partner], with no reduction modulo lattice translations. For a unit_cell or molecular_unit_cell span, operation_xyz is a lattice translation x+n1,y+n2,z+n3 with integers n1, n2, n3 (zero along non-periodic directions), and the entry stands for that bond and its translates by every lattice vector. For the four fundamental-domain and asymmetric-unit spans, the entry stands for its orbit: for every operation g in space_group_symmetry_operations_xyz and every lattice translation t, the atom at g(a) + t is bonded to the atom at g(b) + t, where a and b are the two atoms the entry names. Each bond, or bond orbit, SHOULD be listed once. An atom is never bonded to itself. Where served positions are stated to limited precision, the operation locates the bonded atom to that precision.

    • shapes: REQUIRED; List of strings or null, or null. One entry per site, in the order of species_at_sites, naming the arrangement of the bonds around each atom of that site: a symbol that ends in the number of atoms bonded to one atom of the site. An entry is null when the site has no assigned shape: always for a site with fewer than two bonds, and otherwise when the assignment gave it none, in which case a client may determine one itself. The value is null when no shapes are assigned.

    • source: REQUIRED; Dictionary or null. Who assigned the bonds; null when unknown.

    • shapes_source: REQUIRED; Dictionary or null. Who assigned the shapes, in the form of source; null when source assigned them or no shapes are assigned.
