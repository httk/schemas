Bond network and coordination polyhedra (property)

This page documents an OPTIMADE Property Definition. See https://schemas.optimade.org/ for more information.

ID: https://schemas.httk.org/defs/v0.1/properties/chemistry/bonds
Definition name: bonds

Property name: Bond network and coordination polyhedra
Description: The bond network of a structure and the coordination polyhedra of its sites: which atoms are bonded to which, and the shape of the bonds around the atoms of a site, which besides coordination polyhedra also covers linear, bent and planar arrangements; the default assignment of httk gives a shape to every site with two or more bonds. The value also names who assigned the bonds and the shapes.
Type: dictionary
Implementation requirements:
- Support: OPTIONAL support in implementations, i.e., MAY be null.

  • Query: Support for queries on this property is OPTIONAL.
  • Response:

The network is stated in terms of the served sites of the structure and the served symmetry operations, so a client needs only fractional_site_positions (or cartesian_site_positions and lattice_vectors), site_coordinate_span and space_group_symmetry_operations_xyz of the same response. What counts as a bond is decided by whoever assigned the network, named in source.

Requirements/Conventions:

  • The value MUST be null when no bond network is known, and when site_coordinate_span is not one of unit_cell, molecular_unit_cell, fundamental_domain, molecular_fundamental_domain, asymmetric_unit and molecular_asymmetric_unit.

  • Otherwise it MUST be a dictionary with the following keys:

    • pairs: REQUIRED; List of Lists of 6 Integers. The bonds, one row [site, partner, op, t1, t2, t3] per bond, or per bond orbit for a symmetric span; an empty list means the structure is known to have no bonds. site and partner are 0-based indices into the sites of the structure (the order of species_at_sites and fractional_site_positions), op is a 0-based index into space_group_symmetry_operations_xyz of the same entry, and t1, t2, t3 are integers.

    • Reading rule: the atom at fractional_site_positions[site] is bonded to the atom at g(fractional_site_positions[partner]) + (t1, t2, t3), where g is the operation space_group_symmetry_operations_xyz[op] applied to fractional coordinates, with no reduction modulo lattice translations. The property can therefore only be read together with space_group_symmetry_operations_xyz of the same response, and a client SHOULD request both. That list holds every operation of the space group for the served cell, centring translations included, as the OPTIMADE standard requires; every bond operation MUST be one of its entries followed by a lattice translation, and a network that cannot be stated this way MUST be served as null.

    • When space_group_symmetry_operations_xyz is null (no symmetry is served, or the structure is not periodic), op MUST be 0 and means the identity x,y,z.
    • For a unit_cell or molecular_unit_cell span, op MUST name the entry x,y,z of space_group_symmetry_operations_xyz, or be 0 when that list is null, and the bond's translation is (t1, t2, t3) (zero along non-periodic directions); the row stands for that bond and its translates by every lattice vector.
    • For the four fundamental-domain and asymmetric-unit spans, the row stands for its orbit: for every operation h in space_group_symmetry_operations_xyz and every lattice translation t, the atom at h(a) + t is bonded to the atom at h(b) + t, where a and b are the two atoms the row names.
    • Each bond, or bond orbit, SHOULD be listed once. An atom is never bonded to itself.

    • Where served positions are stated to limited precision, the row locates the bonded atom to that precision.

    • shapes: REQUIRED; Dictionary or null. The assigned shapes, each the arrangement of the bonds around one atom of a site: a coordination polyhedron, or a linear, bent or planar arrangement. A dictionary of aligned lists with one entry per assigned shape:

    • sites: REQUIRED; List of Integers. The 0-based index of the centre site of each shape.

    • symbols: REQUIRED; List of Strings. The symbol of each shape (see the symbols key below).
    • pair_rows: OPTIONAL; List of entries that are a List of Integers or null, or null. The rows of pairs each shape covers.

    The lists present MUST have the same length, and sites and symbols MUST NOT contain null. A site that does not appear in sites has no assigned shape: always a site with fewer than two bonds, and otherwise a site the assignment gave none, in which case a client may determine one itself. The value is null when no shapes are assigned.

    • Coverage rule: a shape whose pair_rows entry is null, or whose pair_rows list is null or absent, covers every row of pairs that names its site in either column; for a symmetric span every bond of the orbit of such a row at an atom of the site. This is the normal case.
    • A pair_rows entry that is a list of 0-based row indices into pairs limits the shape to those rows. Each listed row MUST name the site in one of its two columns. In a symmetric span a row brings in every bond of its orbit at that site.
    • The number that ends a named symbol MUST equal the number of distinct atoms bonded to one atom of the site through the rows the shape covers. The irregular symbol I carries no number: its count is the number of bonded atoms (bonds) the shape covers, at least 3. For a unit-cell span these atoms can be counted from the rows: a row [i, j, op, t1, t2, t3] with i different from j adds one atom at each end, and a row [i, i, op, t1, t2, t3] adds two atoms at i.
    • A site MAY appear more than once in sites only when the pair_rows entries of all its shapes are lists that share no row.

    • Illustration: a centre site 1 with six bonds forming an octahedron (row 0, a bond orbit of a symmetric span) and a second bond orbit (row 1) that is left out of the shape:

      "pairs": [[1, 2, 0, 0, 0, 0], [1, 1, 0, 1, 0, 0]], "shapes": {"sites": [1], "symbols": ["OC6"], "pair_rows": [[0]]}

    • source: REQUIRED; Dictionary or null. Who assigned the bonds; null when unknown.

    • shapes_source: REQUIRED; Dictionary or null. Who assigned the shapes, in the form of source; null when source assigned them or no shapes are assigned.

  • Layout rule: each kind of bond is one matrix of integer rows, and no bond is a nested object. Bonds of three or more atoms would get a field of their own (for example triples), and attributes of pair bonds would be lists aligned with the rows of pairs.

  • Not covered: polyhedra without a central atom (cages). A shape over only some of the bonds of its centre is covered by pair_rows.
